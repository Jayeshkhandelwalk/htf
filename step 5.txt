For your AI-based agricultural by-product analyser, we can add fibre content, microbial safety, and microbial quality as three analysis modules. Microbial safety and microbial quality overlap, but they are not exactly the same.

# 1. Fibre content detection

Fibre analysis helps determine whether a by-product is suitable for a protein-rich food ingredient. High fibre may affect texture, digestibility and protein concentration.

[Dietary Fiber Analyzer](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/Qy-NbiJMCcH91UgqK5bnt-YBXntaepS4RWe5cyAembY-zOuWrp5pqSHMLl3cyql8LyQompi0ViEFhN9zVl79iT6sJzYg6Ww6-8IxQorYJjtHmqCCJMmLXn5F5UYt5Mhyq3YIV7N4hofoLRPVVz2iO4xdqerevfx-i0vRmJ328Xk?purpose=inline)

What to measure

- Total dietary fibre (TDF)
- Soluble dietary fibre
- Insoluble dietary fibre
- Crude fibre, if required for comparison with existing feed data

## How to measure fibre

| Method                         | Purpose                                                             |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Enzymatic-gravimetric analysis | Reference measurement of dietary fibre                              |
| Crude fibre analysis           | Measures a narrower fraction; not equivalent to total dietary fibre |
| NIR spectroscopy               | Estimates fibre from calibrated spectral data                       |
| NIR + AI regression            | Predicts fibre percentage using laboratory-labelled samples         |

For human-food applications, enzymatic-gravimetric methods such as applicable AOAC dietary-fibre methods are generally more relevant than crude fibre alone.

### Hardware required

- NIR spectrometer for rapid estimation.
- Analytical balance, drying oven and suitable laboratory equipment.
- Access to a laboratory with the reagents and equipment needed for the selected reference method.
- Laptop to run the prediction model.

AI model: Start with Partial Least Squares Regression (PLSR), then compare it with Support Vector Regression (SVR).

Output: predicted total dietary fibre, soluble fibre and insoluble fibre, each in % dry matter if that is the chosen reporting basis.

Important: train separate models for each fibre measure. A crude-fibre result cannot simply be used as the ground truth for total dietary fibre.

# 2. Microbial safety

Microbial safety asks whether a sample meets the relevant microbiological safety requirements for its intended use. It is not established simply by having a low total microbial count.

[Real-Time PCR 食品病原菌檢測系統 | 日澤國際股份有限公司](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/SLQHkS5J7C_5VsSO43DYrCU5ztPbIiwAQZYofncnJvShwoOnSpqAwvQCOLY12InVrGm3z5ZisZz63wwPmwcJvFKQ-lgYmjFCF7X9uzjPWDFE80bXQnU2A1ZvoFO6juFxDUkCEPhViSxzLHleH8Rcx1WLcD3oQIRUkgl3wvBtgXg?purpose=inline)

What to assess

- Presence or absence of specified pathogens.
- Relevant pathogen counts where required.
- Compliance with the applicable food category and testing standard.
- Whether the sample needs confirmatory laboratory testing.

## Testing methods

| Method                          | What it tells you                                                  |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Culture-based reference methods | Whether target organisms grow under specified test conditions      |
| PCR/qPCR                        | Detects genetic material from selected organisms                   |
| Validated rapid immunoassays    | Detect selected pathogens or their targets                         |
| ATP bioluminescence             | Indicates residual biological material; not a direct pathogen test |

Depending on the product and applicable requirements, testing may include organisms such as Salmonella or other specified pathogens.

### Hardware required

- Appropriate laboratory microbiology equipment or validated commercial rapid-test systems.
- PCR/qPCR equipment if molecular testing is selected.
- A reader for rapid test kits, where required.
- Laptop or server for recording results and generating reports.

AI role: interpret validated test outputs, flag results requiring confirmation, and combine test records with traceability and storage history.

AI should not independently declare a sample pathogen-free based on temperature, humidity, colour or an ordinary image.

# 3. Microbial quality

Microbial quality describes the broader microbial condition of the material, including indicators of spoilage and the general microbial load.

[Kolonien automatisch zählen](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/I0We3gKH86KeCwFq3txECKtL7tz6OfGXeKlphVvgMZ_47tRlJGRR3rn5zdDq_YkehSXkqIQLwSqBTN0V8KRfv1VMflalIjvUsY6NWpvQhqev1lW7J6cKgVO2r7rhLNQhVw8wYcDfruRjxl6EDs4ikE1gpqClGJw8sboi1jWyuh4?purpose=inline)

What to measure

- Aerobic plate count / total viable count.
- Yeast and mould counts.
- Other indicator organisms appropriate to the product.
- Moisture and water activity as supporting storage-risk indicators.

## Testing methods

| Method                      | Application                                                   |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Standard plate count        | Estimates viable organisms under specified conditions         |
| Yeast and mould enumeration | Estimates fungal load                                         |
| Water activity meter        | Measures water available to support microbial growth          |
| Moisture analyser           | Measures moisture content, which influences storage stability |

A sample can have a high microbial count without containing a particular pathogen, and a low count does not prove the absence of pathogens or toxins.

### Hardware required

- Colony counter and suitable laboratory equipment for reference plate counts.
- Water activity meter.
- Moisture analyser or validated moisture measurement setup.
- Optional rapid microbiology system.

For a college prototype, use properly obtained laboratory datasets or results from a qualified testing laboratory rather than attempting to culture hazardous microorganisms outside an appropriate lab.

| Dashboard               | HTML, CSS, JavaScript                |
| Visualisation           | Plotly or Chart.js                   |

For microbial safety, rule-based checks against the applicable standards are often more defensible than a general machine-learning classifier. Any predictive model must be independently validated and must not replace required laboratory tests.

## 6. Final testing plan

For your first prototype, I would prioritise:

1. Fibre: measure total dietary fibre in the laboratory and train an NIR calibration model.
2. Microbial quality: collect reference plate counts, yeast/mould results, moisture and water-activity readings.
3. Microbial safety: integrate validated pathogen-test results and flag samples that need confirmation.

This gives you a useful division between AI-estimated composition and laboratory-verified safety—two different functions that should remain separate in the final report.
