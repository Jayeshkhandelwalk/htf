For your agricultural by-product analyser, the next three parameters are aflatoxin contamination, microbial quality, and allergen presence. Unlike protein and residual oil, these are primarily food-safety checks, so your system must be much more careful about false negatives.

The best design is a hybrid AI system: use sensors or rapid screening tests for initial assessment, laboratory methods for reference results, and AI to interpret validated measurements. Do not rely on an ordinary camera or NIR spectrum alone to declare a sample safe.

# 1. Aflatoxin detection

[Aflatoxin Analysis in Peanuts](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/f3hHyijCX68VJFfUDjHTo3dRax2cEQg71CWwXU2F9UZGeJdcA9fOoIieldEg7dAfDXZ0xC2JgAzntDhZJtrn59bKtgVBiQNSAjh8idV6BPsikXamhHAz5RGuEYShJMvkHq992kjWwtaU0L_ep9qlDWWFhBA-SXE64JRk3a0K0M4?purpose=inline)

Aflatoxins are toxic compounds produced by certain moulds, particularly Aspergillus flavus and Aspergillus parasiticus. They are especially relevant to groundnut cake and can occur in other improperly stored crops and by-products.

Target: measure aflatoxin concentration, commonly AFB1 and/or total aflatoxins, in units such as µg/kg.

## How to measure it

| Method                           | What it does                                     | Role in your system                         |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| ELISA                            | Detects specific aflatoxins using antibodies     | Practical screening method                  |
| Lateral-flow immunoassay         | Rapid, kit-based detection                       | Potential portable screening                |
| HPLC with fluorescence detection | Quantifies aflatoxins after suitable preparation | Laboratory reference or confirmatory method |
| LC-MS/MS                         | Identifies and quantifies specific toxins        | Advanced confirmatory analysis              |

AI opportunity: combine sensor or test-kit readings with laboratory-confirmed aflatoxin concentrations to build a regression model for concentration or a classification model for screening categories.

NIR alone is not a dependable direct aflatoxin detector at low concentrations. Spectral changes associated with mould damage may not reliably indicate the toxin itself.

# 2. Microbial quality

[Como montar um plano de amostragem microbiológica conforme a IN 161: fundamentos, aplicação prática e desafios contemporâneos](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/QB69ducNPbAdEY25hfzIXJKfG_hjmzl1l3jC9YWz-VsYjU6vaOORJYnrTddDBtFerGgC00aOY82XFImYxyrFgqDV1UnfvOhPyvtcEax4Tnn6vOKk2oZnMkF2WbNOUq1vW0HSegT2CmKs960X5QicY5Per4qJapCXFXRLWeWxUOQ?purpose=inline)

Microbial quality indicates whether a sample has an acceptable microbial load and whether relevant pathogens or spoilage organisms are present.

Targets to consider:

- Total viable count / aerobic plate count
- Yeasts and moulds
- Salmonella detection where relevant
- Other organisms based on the product, processing conditions and applicable standards

## How to measure it

| Method                      | What it tells you                                                      |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Standard plate count        | Viable microorganisms able to grow under the specified test conditions |
| Yeast and mould enumeration | Counts of relevant fungi                                               |
| PCR or qPCR                 | Detects target microbial genetic material                              |
| Validated rapid immunoassay | Detects specific targets, depending on the kit                         |

A plate count measures organisms capable of growing under the test conditions; it does not detect every microorganism. Likewise, PCR detection of genetic material does not always establish that an organism is alive.

AI opportunity: use validated plate counts, molecular test results and relevant environmental measurements to estimate microbial load or classify samples for further testing.

Temperature and humidity sensors can help monitor storage conditions and identify spoilage risks, but they cannot independently confirm that a sample is microbiologically safe.

For a college prototype, you can use existing, properly anonymised laboratory datasets instead of culturing potentially hazardous organisms yourself.

# 3. Allergen presence

[Food Allergen Detection ELISA Kit Price,Manufacturer Supplier,Exporter,in New Delhi](https://images.openai.com/static-rsc-4/sXQiIYyk48h2ydKSyecmaSpaGaSwB_lI60MKBBh2p9eobPTkvsUFI0faN7OZ5I3tcBN8LThqW2m7EwjB5_39CnZJbN0976pXdiNattzXI4hFF9ZIV7HxhFUYwow9xsAz1P1QWOTVgh8fE33h94dbYZYHA4uYn4-uJk50_0gXNUY?purpose=inline)

Some by-products contain allergenic proteins even after oil extraction or other processing.

Important examples:

- Groundnut cake: peanut allergens
- Almond cake: almond allergens
- Pulse residues: potential allergens associated with the specific pulse species
- Shared processing equipment: possible cross-contact with other allergenic ingredients

## How to measure it

| Method                              | What it detects                                         | Main limitation                                    |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| ELISA                               | Specific allergenic proteins or epitopes                | Heat and processing can affect detection           |
| Targeted LC-MS/MS                   | Selected allergen-associated peptides                   | Requires specialist analysis and validated methods |
| PCR                                 | DNA from a particular plant species                     | Does not directly quantify allergenic protein      |
| Label and supply-chain verification | Known ingredient identity and cross-contact information | Cannot independently prove absence                 |

AI opportunity: combine assay results, ingredient identity, supplier records and processing history to produce an allergen-risk report.

There is an important distinction between detecting an allergenic ingredient and measuring its allergenic potential. A negative result from one test does not guarantee the complete absence of allergens, and processing may alter how well a particular test detects them.

For your project, treat allergens as a separate safety module rather than trying to predict them from the same NIR model used for protein.

## 5. AI and software stack

| Component                      | Suggested technology                   |
| ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------- |
| Test reader / sensor interface | Python, device SDK or serial interface |
| Data processing                | pandas, NumPy, SciPy                   |
| AI models                      | scikit-learn                           |
| Concentration prediction       | PLSR, SVR or suitable regression model |
| Risk classification            | Logistic regression, Random Forest     |
| Backend                        | FastAPI                                |
| Database                       | SQLite initially                       |
| Dashboard                      | HTML, CSS, JavaScript                  |
| Data visualisation             | Plotly or Chart.js                     |

Use separate models for separate targets. For example, aflatoxin concentration prediction is a regression task, while identifying whether a particular allergen was detected is a classification task.

## 6. Updated food-safety system flowchart

Agricultural by-product sample

Sample ID, source, batch and storage history

Divide into testing modules

Aflatoxins

ELISA / rapid test

Microbes

Counts / PCR

Allergens

ELISA / targeted analysis

Reference results + quality-controlled dataset

Validated laboratory measurements and known detection limits

AI prediction and decision support

Concentration, detection status, uncertainty and retest flags

Food-safety review

Compare confirmed results with applicable standards; escalate uncertain or positive findings

The final output should distinguish “not detected by this test” from “absent” and “safe for human consumption.” A sample should not be cleared for food use by an AI prediction alone.

## 7. Best implementation strategy

I would develop the full system in two stages:

- Stage 1 — Composition analyser: protein, moisture, residual oil and, where feasible, residual gum.
- Stage 2 — Safety screening: aflatoxin testing, microbial quality and allergen-specific detection.

This gives you a credible project combining spectroscopy, machine learning, laboratory analysis and food-safety decision support without pretending one sensor can detect everything.

For Indian food applications, check the current applicable requirements from the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India ([FSSAI official website](https://www.fssai.gov.in/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)) and use appropriate validated testing methods.
